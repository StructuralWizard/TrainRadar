Live Spanish train delays: how to check if your Renfe train is late

Updated 10 November 2026 · TrainRadar (Metranos Ltd)

TrainRadar is a live map of Renfe trains across Spain. Every train shows where it is right now, coloured by its delay: green on time, amber from 5 minutes late and red from 15. It's free and runs in your browser, in English or Spanish.

Open the live map

How to check your train

  1. Open mytrainradar.com on your phone or computer (tap EN for English).
  2. Search for the train number (for example 03021), the line (C5, R2…), a station or a city. You can also search from origin to destination: "Madrid to Seville".
  3. Tap the train to see its delay in minutes, direction, speed and next stop.
  4. Tap a station to see its next departures and the trains approaching it, with their delays.

Which trains it covers

  • Cercanías and Rodalies commuter trains: every Renfe network (Madrid, Barcelona's Rodalies de Catalunya, Valencia, Seville, Málaga, Bilbao, Asturias and the rest), each line in its own colour.
  • High-speed and long-distance: AVE, Avlo, Alvia, Euromed and Intercity.
  • Regional (Media Distancia) trains.
  • OUIGO, from its published timetable (no live delays).

Where the data comes from

TrainRadar uses the open real-time data that Renfe Operadora publishes at data.renfe.com (CC BY 4.0): train positions, delays and service notices. OUIGO timetables come from the Spanish Ministry of Transport's National Access Point. When Renfe gives no position for a train, TrainRadar estimates it from the timetable and says so.

TrainRadar isn't a Renfe site and isn't affiliated with Renfe, Adif or OUIGO. Data can be late or wrong: for anything important, check Renfe's official channels.

Notices and punctuality

Renfe's service notices (engineering works, strikes, replacement buses, incidents) show on the map and in a list. TrainRadar records every train's delay, and shows each line's punctuality over the last 7 days for free. TrainRadar Pro (Android app, €1.99 a year) extends the history to 12 months and adds My trips, which logs the delay of each journey you take, handy for claiming compensation.

FAQ

How can I check if my Renfe train is delayed right now?
Search for the train number, line or station at mytrainradar.com. The train shows on the map with its delay in minutes and in colour.
Is TrainRadar an official Renfe site?
No. It's an independent project using Renfe's open data. Renfe's own official information is on renfe.com and in its app.
Is it free? Do I need an account?
It's free, with no account, no cookies and no ads.
Does it work for Barcelona's Rodalies?
Yes: every Cercanías and Rodalies network that Renfe operates and publishes in real time.
Which trains does it cover?
Every train Renfe publishes in real time across Spain: Cercanías and Rodalies commuter trains, AVE, Avlo, Alvia, Euromed, Intercity and regional (Media Distancia) trains. OUIGO trains are shown by timetable, without live delays.
