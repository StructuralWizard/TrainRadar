# 3.3.1: All of Portugal

- **Portugal, the whole country**: CP's trains across Portugal (Alfa Pendular, Intercidades, InterRegional, Regional and the Lisbon, Porto and Coimbra urban lines) join Lisbon's Fertagus and Metro: 521 stations, placed by timetable (Portugal publishes no live data). The country is now simply "Portugal" and opens on the whole map.

Data: CP – Comboios de Portugal timetable (publico.cp.pt), Fertagus (CC BY 4.0), Metropolitano de Lisboa (dados.gov.pt, CC BY). Track: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Not affiliated with CP, Fertagus or Metropolitano de Lisboa.
