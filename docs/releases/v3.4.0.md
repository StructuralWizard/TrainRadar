# 3.4.0: A new look, and cab rides

- **A new look**: a cleaner design with the Inter typeface and light and dark themes. Trains are discs coloured by delay, with an arrow for their heading and a label with the line and delay. On a phone the app opens on the map; on a wide screen the details sit in a side panel next to it.
- **Train details**: one view on phone and desktop. A pill shows the delay, a timeline shows the stops, and cards show the trains ahead and behind when there are any. **Follow** keeps the train in view, and next time the app offers to follow it again.
- **Stations**: departures and arrivals for now, today, tomorrow or another day, with the line badges.
- **Every country, its own way**: the title, layers, key and About page follow the chosen country. The area picker is readable in dark mode and says "Areas" and "Find an area" outside Spain.
- **Videos from the cab**: about 2,500 YouTube cab-ride videos in 21 countries. A station lists the videos that pass it and opens YouTube at the moment the train gets there. A train offers "Watch from the cab" at the point where it is now. Free, no thumbnails, and videos YouTube no longer serves are left out each week.

Data: national timetables and live feeds as listed on each country's About page. Cab-ride videos belong to their uploaders on YouTube; TrainRadar only links to them. Track: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
