# Phase A6: notices

Renfe's service notices (GTFS-RT alerts) are now in the app.

- "⚠ Avisos (N)" button and panel: each notice with its Cercanías region, lines and stations, and the date it started; Renfe's `#tags` become line labels
- Stations named in notices are marked on the map; tap one to see its notices
- A train's popup says when there are notices on its line and opens them
- The panel shows when Renfe last updated the feed, and warns when it's more than 2 days old (on 1 Nov 2026 it hadn't changed since 7 Oct)
- Link to Adif's "Estado de la red" page. Adif publishes no feed and its terms forbid reuse without permission, so TrainRadar doesn't scrape it (see docs/data-sources.md)
- Notice text is shown as plain text only
