# 2.7: Search from A to B, zones and a clearer map

- Search from origin to destination: type "Madrid a Sevilla" (or "de Bilbao a León", "Sants → Girona") and see the direct trains of the next 4 hours with their delay. A city counts all its main stations. Stations also get a "Trenes a…" button.
- The search finds cities, stations, provinces and autonomous communities, and forgives accents, word order and small typos ("sevlla", "chamartn").
- Zones: "Ir a…" and the area picker list every autonomous community and province after the Cercanías networks.
- A clearer map on phones:
  - a colour per service: purple for high speed, teal for conventional, each Cercanías line in its own colour;
  - thicker lines with a white border;
  - stations as white circles, interchanges bigger and named sooner;
  - trains as larger drops pointing where they're heading, with "C5 +12" labels when you zoom into a city;
  - a full key in Capas (tap the delay chip).
- "Retrasos ahora" can be pulled down out of the way; a small button brings it back.
- Notices can show only those about your favourites and saved commutes.
- Favourites and commutes: an "Añadir" button to search and add a station, a train or an origin → destination pair.
- Más now holds the Pro tools: Mis viajes, Moviola, Trayecto habitual, delay alerts and the widget.
- Left-handed mode (Más → Modo zurdo) moves the map buttons to the left.
- The map credits (ⓘ) close again with a tap, and shared links point to mytrainradar.com.
