# Phase A3: engine, Worker and OpenStreetMap track

The browser engine now turns Renfe's live feeds into trains on the map. There's
no map UI yet (that's A4).

- GTFS-RT decoding for vehicle positions, trip updates and alerts, with Renfe specifics behind the `SourceAdapter` trait
- Trip matching by trip id, then train number; duplicate listings merged
- Delays from Renfe's trip updates (99% of trains), or estimated from GPS
- Interpolation between GPS fixes along the track, with fix age
- Spacing to the train ahead and behind on the same line, in km and minutes
- Poll scheduler: 30 s, jitter, backoff, paused in hidden tabs
- AV/LD/MD trains follow OpenStreetMap track (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)
- First load 138 KB gzipped; track geometry loads afterwards
- Cloudflare Worker: CORS pass-through for the five Renfe feeds (deployed in A7)
- `live_check` runs the engine against current Renfe feeds in CI

Data: © Renfe Operadora, CC BY 4.0. Track: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Not affiliated with Renfe or Adif.
