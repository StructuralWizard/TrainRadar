# 3.2: Every country, your daily trips first, and home-screen icons

- **Every country**: Spain and the UK, plus Ireland, France, Luxembourg, Finland, Norway, the Netherlands, Slovakia, Estonia, Switzerland, Sweden, Poland, Austria, Czechia, Italy, Denmark, Hungary, Germany, Belgium, Australia (New South Wales, Victoria), New Zealand (Wellington, Auckland) and Singapore (estimated positions). Live delays where the operators publish them; elsewhere, by timetable.
- **Country and area drop-downs**: pick the country, then its area (Cercanías networks, communities and provinces in Spain; regions, states and cities elsewhere). The app opens on the last country and area you used, and each country keeps its own map view and favourites.
- **Go to another country from the map**: drag the map to a neighbouring country and tap it, then "Ir a …".
- **Works offline**: a country's timetables download the first time you open it and stay on the phone.
- **Your daily trips first (app)**: with favourites saved, the app opens on Favoritos, listed as stations, then your usual commutes, then trains.
- **Home-screen icons (TrainRadar Pro, Android)**: put a favourite station, train or commute on the home screen as an icon that opens it directly. Long-press the app icon for your first four favourites.
- **All countries at a glance (website)**: zoom out to see every country with how many trains have live data now; click one to go there.

Sweden's timetable may be missing for a few days at the start of the month while Trafiklab's download quota renews.
