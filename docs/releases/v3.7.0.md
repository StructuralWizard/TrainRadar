# 3.7.0: Share a heads-up, OUIGO and AVE on station boards, the world view in the app

- **Share a heads-up** ("Compartir aviso"): on any train, pick what's happening (running late, stopped, hasn't turned up, very crowded, an incident) and send it to your own chats through the share menu, with where the train is and its official delay. Nothing is sent to TrainRadar.
- **Station boards keep the long-distance trains**: at stations that Cercanías also serve (Madrid-Chamartín, Atocha, Barcelona-Sants, Málaga, Sevilla…), the first rows used to fill with commuter trains, so OUIGO, AVE, Avlo and Alvia fell off the list. Now the long-distance trains of the next 3 hours are always listed after the next trains, in departures, arrivals and plan ahead.
- **The world view in the app**: zoom out to see every country's network and how many trains are running, as on the website; tap a country to go there.
- **New languages**: Chinese in traditional characters for Taiwan and for Hong Kong (Simplified stays for the mainland), and Tagalog (Filipino). TrainRadar now speaks 21 languages.
- **Travellers' reports (UK)**: the app is ready for travellers to tell each other about delays, trains that haven't come and serious incidents, checked on the phone without sending its location. Not switched on yet.
