# 2.5: Replay

- TrainRadar Pro (Android): ⏪ Moviola. Pick a day and a time and see where every train was, coloured by how late it was; play it back at ×1, ×10, ×60 or ×300, or scrub through the day; tap a train for its delay at that moment. "Volver al directo" (or Back) returns to the live map.
- The server now keeps a picture of the map every minute (every train's position, direction and delay) in hourly files, from the day this version's server goes live. Train data only, nothing about people. About 3–10 MB a day.
