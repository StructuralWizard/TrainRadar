# 1.2: gradients

- Popup: track gradient at the train ("subida 12 ‰", "bajada 8 ‰", "llano") and its altitude
- A small height profile of the whole route, with the train's position on it
- Heights come from the Copernicus terrain model (30 m), with tunnels and bridges interpolated between their ends, so gradients are approximate
- AV/LD/MD and OUIGO trains for now; Cercanías on Renfe's own shapes later
