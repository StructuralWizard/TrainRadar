# 2.9: Notices history, a readable moviola and your commutes in Favoritos

- Notices history (TrainRadar Pro): Avisos → "Histórico de avisos" shows the Renfe notices (works, strikes, disruptions) active on any past day, with when each was seen. The history starts on the day this version's server change went live.
- Moviola: the train's popup and the control bar are readable in dark mode. New ⏪ and ⏩ buttons go 5 minutes back and forward, and the Back button closes the moviola at once.
- Favoritos lists your usual commutes first (Pro); one tap opens their next trains.
- A small "PRO" sticker on the map when TrainRadar Pro is active.
