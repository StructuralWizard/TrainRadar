# 3.1: Journeys with changes, saved trains with alerts, and timetables weeks ahead

- **Cómo llegar with changes**: from one station or city to another, direct or with up to two changes, with live delays. You see the change time at each station, the short walk between nearby stations (Atocha Cercanías ↔ Puerta de Atocha, London's terminals), and "enlace en riesgo" when a delay leaves less than five minutes. In Spain and the UK.
- **Save a journey's trains (TrainRadar Pro)**: "Guardar en mis trayectos" keeps those exact trains on the commute, with its days. The commute screen shows today's state of each train and change, and "Ver otras opciones" if one is cancelled or a connection breaks.
- **Commute alerts (Pro, Android)**: for each saved journey, on its days, you're notified of delays (5 and 15 min) and cancellations, a connection at risk or missed, a reminder before leaving, platform changes, and Renfe's notices at your stations (lifts, works, replacement buses). Each kind can be switched off. For journeys in Spain.
- **Timetables weeks ahead**: choose a date and time for a station's departures and arrivals, for Cómo llegar, or for a train's stops (search its number, then "otro día"). Up to nine weeks ahead, as far as Renfe, Network Rail and Translink publish. By timetable, without live data.
