# 3.3: 18 languages, more areas, and the app starts in your country

- **18 languages**: Spanish, English, Portuguese, Japanese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Estonian and Chinese (Simplified). The first time, the app follows your phone's or browser's language; More → Language lists them all, each in its own name. Notifications and widgets follow the app's language.
- **Starts in your country (app)**: on its first start the app opens on the country where the phone is, from its SIM or network.
- **Country list**: your current country first, then every other one A–Z in your language.
- **More areas**: the UK's nations and regions, and the regions, states and provinces of every country, as areas to zoom to.
- **New shortcut icons (TrainRadar Pro, Android)**: clearer home-screen icons for a station and a commute.
- **Portugal and Japan (in testing)**: Lisbon's trains by timetable, and Toei's trains in Tokyo with live positions, open to testers by link; not in the country list yet.
- **Denmark**: DSB's trains, lines and live delays.
- **Singapore**: the MRT and LRT lines now show on the map.
- **All countries at a glance (website)**: zoomed out, each country's network, its number of stations and lines, and how many trains are running now, refreshed every ten minutes.
