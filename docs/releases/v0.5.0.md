# Phase A5: your location

Location is off until you tap "Mi ubicación". It's used only in your browser and is never sent, logged or stored.

- Blue dot with accuracy circle, and a "centre on me" button
- "Near you": the closest stations, next trains at the nearest one (timetable order, live delays, cancellations), and trains approaching it with ETA and distance
- "Am I on this train?": compares your movement over 30–90 s with nearby trains and reports a confidence (high / medium), or says it can't tell yet
- Clear messages for denied permission, timeouts, low accuracy and browsers without location support; everything else keeps working
- Location stops when you turn it off or leave the tab; fixes are forgotten after 90 s
- Browser test in CI with emulated GPS: nearby stations, permission denied, and riding along with a train
