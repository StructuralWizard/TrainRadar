# 2.9.1: Station boards that don't run out, and upcoming works

- Fix: station departures and arrivals went empty about a week after installing or updating the app (and in a browser tab left open for days). The timetable in the app only covered a week. The app now downloads the current timetable from mytrainradar.com when it starts, checks for a new one every 30 minutes and when you come back to it, and uses its own copy when offline.
- "Próximas obras" in Avisos (free): Renfe's notices that haven't started yet, soonest first, below the current ones.
- National Rail's notices on the website now credit "National Rail", as the Rail Data Marketplace asked. The United Kingdom stays on the website only for now; the app keeps showing Spain until 3.0.
