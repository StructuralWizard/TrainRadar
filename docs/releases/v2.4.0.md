# 2.4: where the history starts, and Renfe's official figures

- History screen: a note says TrainRadar has been recording delays since 4 November 2026, and when the chosen period starts earlier, that the earlier days have no data. A short history no longer looks like a fault.
- History screen: a link to Renfe's official monthly punctuality figures for the service (Cercanías, AVE and Larga Distancia, or Media Distancia; the general page for stations). They're worked out another way (cancelled trains aren't counted), so they're linked, not mixed into TrainRadar's figures.
- A train just chosen (tap, search or link) now opens its card from the top, not where a previous card had been scrolled to.
