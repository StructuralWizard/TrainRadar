# 2.4: no more ghost trains at night, any train's history, Renfe's official figures

- History screen: a note says TrainRadar has been recording delays since 4 November 2026, and when the chosen period starts earlier, that the earlier days have no data. A short history no longer looks like a fault.
- History screen: a link to Renfe's official monthly punctuality figures for the service (Cercanías, AVE and Larga Distancia, or Media Distancia; the general page for stations). They're worked out another way (cancelled trains aren't counted), so they're linked, not mixed into TrainRadar's figures.
- A train just chosen (tap, search or link) now opens its card from the top, not where a previous card had been scrolled to.
- Fix: long-distance and regional trains "hours late" at night. Renfe's AV/LD/MD GPS feed gives positions no time, and keeps listing a train's last position after it has gone; an app just opened took that position as fresh, so the train looked to be on its way with a delay growing every minute (seen: MD 13911 and 13946 at 03:20, +170 and +233 min). The server now publishes since when each position has stayed put (`rt/fixes.tsv`); the app uses it, and a position frozen for over an hour on a trip that should have ended over 30 minutes ago is left off the map. The recorder uses the same times, so these runs no longer enter the history with huge delays.
- TrainRadar Pro: search any train number; if it isn't running now, open its history (its line worked out from its recorded runs).
- The "★ Guardado" and "Registrando este viaje" buttons were coloured text on blue; now dark green on light green.
