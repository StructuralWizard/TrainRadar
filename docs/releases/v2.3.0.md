# 2.3: my trips complete themselves

- Android app, TrainRadar Pro, My trips: if the app was closed when your train arrived, the arrival delay (and the delay at each stop) is now filled in from TrainRadar's history, which records every run. Open the app any time after the trip; there's no need to keep it open until the end.
- The CSV export has Spanish headings when the app is in Spanish, separated by ";" so Excel opens it in columns.
- History API: `/v1/history/train/<n>?today=1` also returns today's finished runs (cached 5 minutes).
