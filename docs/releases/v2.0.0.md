# 2.0: central live data, delay history and the Android app

- One central poller fetches Renfe's feeds for everyone; the site and the app read them from live.mytrainradar.com (cached), with the old pass-through as fallback
- Delay history: every run recorded; punctuality per line, train and station in the history API (api.mytrainradar.com); "last 7 days" in train popups and a history screen with charts
- Favourites (trains and stations), sharing, links to a train or station (`?train=`, `?station=`)
- Android app (Capacitor): the map and timetables inside the app, the back button, `trainradar://` links, Android backup of favourites
- TrainRadar Pro (Android, monthly subscription; tips): unlimited favourites, delay alerts with the app closed, a home-screen widget, my trips with a claim summary, CSV and image, full history up to 12 months, commute mode, track details
- Settings: export, import and delete your data
- Legal pages with Metranos Ltd's details
