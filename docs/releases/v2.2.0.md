# 2.2: Delays and Notices for your commutes

- Android app, TrainRadar Pro: the Delays list and the Notices sheet can show only your commutes. Tap "Todos" or one of your saved commutes at the top; the choice is remembered.
  - Delays: delayed trains that run from your origin to your destination and haven't got there yet.
  - Notices: Renfe notices at any station on the way (both ends included) or on a line your trains run on.
