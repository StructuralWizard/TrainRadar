# 2.10: Widgets for stations, trains and notices; Favoritos your way

- New widgets (TrainRadar Pro): departures or arrivals of a station, one train (delay, next stop, destination) and Renfe's notices. Add as many as you like: each shows the favourite station or train you choose. They update every 15 minutes, or at once with ↻, and a tap opens the station or train.
- The favourite-trains widget no longer drains the battery or flickers.
- The app opens where you left it: same map view, and the train or station you had open.
- Favoritos: trains and stations in separate lists, each sorted as you like (drag ⠿). Swipe a favourite's details sideways to go to the next one.
- A station's details show at once, with the map moving behind them.
- The Delays list's commute filter shows a commute added a moment ago.
