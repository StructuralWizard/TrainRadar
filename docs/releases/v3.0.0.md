# 3.0: Trains in the United Kingdom, with Northern Ireland

- **United Kingdom**: tap "España" at the top of the map and choose Reino Unido (and back). Great Britain's trains are on the map with Network Rail's live delays, placed by timetable and the last reported position ("posición estimada"). Northern Ireland's (NI Railways) are by timetable. Stations show departures and arrivals in London time; Avisos shows National Rail's notices and planned works.
- The UK's timetables download the first time you choose it, so the app stays the same size for Spain.
- Each country keeps its own favourites and commutes. TrainRadar Pro is the same subscription in both.
- Not in the UK yet: the punctuality history, the replay and the notices history (Pro), and delay alerts and widgets, which keep following your trains in Spain.
- The app no longer restarts (losing the open train or station) when Android changes the font size, dark mode or keyboard.
