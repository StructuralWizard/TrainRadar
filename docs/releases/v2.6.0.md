# 2.6: A phone layout made for phones

- On phones (and in the Android app) TrainRadar now looks like an app, not a squeezed website:
  - one search bar at the top (trains, lines and stations) with the network you're looking at;
  - two small chips for the live status and the delay colours;
  - a tab bar: Mapa, Cerca, Avisos, Favoritos, Más.
- Trains and stations open in a sheet you can drag up and down, swipe away or close with Back, instead of a small popup.
- Train sheet:
  - line and delay at a glance;
  - Guardar, Avisarme, Compartir and Historial buttons;
  - the next stops with the timetable time and the expected one.
- Station sheet: departures and arriving trains in large rows; tap one to open its train.
- "Ir a…" becomes a network picker with each Cercanías network's median delay now. "Capas" becomes a sheet of switches.
- On the home screen, "Retrasos ahora" shows how many trains are 15+, 5–14 min late or on time; drag it up for the list.
- Dark mode on phones, larger buttons, SVG icons, and a light tick on Android when a sheet snaps.
- The desktop layout (720 px and wider) is unchanged.
