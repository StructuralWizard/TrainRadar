# 2.8: The whole rail network, by kind of track

- The map now draws Spain's whole rail network from OpenStreetMap, not only the stretches where trains were running: high-speed track in purple, conventional in teal and metre gauge (the former FEVE lines in the north) in brown. Track that is out of use, such as lines closed for works, is drawn faded.
- High-speed and conventional lines that run side by side stay separate, and the lines pass through their stations.
- Trains follow their own kind of track (AVE, Avlo, Avant and OUIGO on high-speed lines; regional trains on conventional ones), so many more of them are placed on the right line.
- The Figueres–Perpignan–Marseille corridor is included.
- "Capas" turns high-speed, conventional and metre-gauge track on and off.
- The page title says what TrainRadar is: "Trenes de Renfe en directo y sus retrasos".
