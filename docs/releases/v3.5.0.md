# 3.5.0: More of Japan

- **Japan's regional lines**: seven new areas, by timetable: Aomori (Kōnan Railway), Yamagata (Flower Nagai Line), Toyama (city tram), Gifu (Akechi Railway), Mie (Ise Railway, with JR Central's Nanki and Mie trains), Tokushima (Asa Seaside Railway's DMV) and Kumamoto (city tram and Kumamoto Electric Railway). Stations are named in English and Japanese, for example "Hirosaki 弘前". Japan is now listed as "Japan".
- **Stations open full screen**: on a phone, a station's departures now fill the screen; drag down for the map.
- **Taiwan, in preparation**: the app is ready for Taiwan's railways and metros (TRA, THSR, the metros) from the national transport data exchange. It isn't offered yet, while the data provider approves access.

Data: Japan's regional lines from their operators via the GTFS Data Repository (gtfs-data.jp): CC BY 4.0 (Kōnan, Yamagata, Akechi, Ise and Asa Seaside railways), CC0 1.0 (Toyama Chihō Railway, Kumamoto Electric Railway) and CC BY 2.1 JP (Kumamoto City Transportation Bureau). Tokyo and Kōchi: Toei and Tosaden Traffic / ODPT, CC BY 4.0. Not affiliated with any operator.
