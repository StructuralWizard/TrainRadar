# 3.6.0: The London Underground, live

- **London Underground, DLR and London Trams**: all 11 Tube lines, the DLR and the trams join the UK map, Night Tube included: 351 stations and about 28,700 trips a week. Their delays are live: every minute TfL's arrival predictions are matched to each train, which is then shown where its timetable puts it, shifted by its delay, in its delay colour.
- **Videos from the cab, on the map**: a new switch in Layers shows the stretches filmed from the cab in fuchsia, over a faded network, with a ring on each filmed station; tap one to watch. The same on phones, tablets, computers and the app; off until you turn it on.
- **UK**: trains placed by timetable are counted as such, no longer all as NI Railways.
