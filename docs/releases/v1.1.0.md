# 1.1: arrows, routes, stopped trains and OUIGO

- Trains are arrows pointing where they're heading
- Tap a train: its route, stops and expected times are drawn, and the other trains on its line are highlighted
- "Tren anterior / siguiente" now works for any stopping pattern and up to 400 km, naming the train: e.g. "C3 20111 · 2 min · 3,2 km"
- Trains stopped between stations get a red ring; the Avisos panel lists them and flags sections with several stopped trains
- Popup: speed (measured or timetabled), gauge and line speed (OpenStreetMap), wheelchair accessibility
- OUIGO trains, placed by timetable from the National Access Point and clearly marked (OUIGO publishes no live data). Powered by MITRAMS
- Cercanías lines in their official colours; lines named in Renfe notices shown dashed orange
- "Ir a…" menu for each núcleo, and layer filters (Cercanías, AV/LD/MD, OUIGO, lines)
- Links to official sources we can't reuse: Adif network status, Renfe Cercanías live, OUIGO, Iryo
